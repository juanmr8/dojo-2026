# 03-scroll-image-scale

What this piece explores: one infinite scroll driving a single playhead across a strip of 40 tiles (the 24 images, repeated apart) exactly the viewport wide. Each lap the scroll sweeps the line left to right; at the right edge it races back on its own, a quick timed rewind the scroll doesn't drive, and the strip reacts both ways. The lens is an area, a wide swell carrying a sharp core: tiles lift off the strip (up or down, several strip heights at the core), drift sideways and swell from their centre, each by its own seeded personality (some wander far, some hold still, some stay on the strip), blended from orderly by one `mess` knob.
